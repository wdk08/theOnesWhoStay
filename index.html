The Ones Who Stay

Written by William Kapadia

Summary

The Ones Who Stay follows Noah, a high school football player with a promising future in the sport. After moving to a new neighborhood, he meets Ava, the chronically ill girl next door, through their adjacent bedroom windows. As their friendship grows into love, Noah discovers that Ava’s condition is worsening. Faced with the choice between the future he’s always dreamed of and the life of the girl he loves, Noah must decide what he’s willing to sacrifice.
